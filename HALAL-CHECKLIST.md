## ملاحظات (Notes)

- Tawali only partners with restaurants that confirm halal food sourcing
- All images of food items are curated to be appropriate and non-deceptive
- The platform does **not** and will **never** feature alcohol, pork, or non-halal products
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is the only payment method — no credit/interest-based transactions
- User consent is obtained before collecting any personal data
- Children under 13 are not permitted to create accounts
- Ads or promotions for prohibited content are strictly forbidden
- The app's terms of service explicitly state halal compliance requirements for listed restaurants
